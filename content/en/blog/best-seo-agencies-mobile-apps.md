## Mobile SEO and ASO: two complementary disciplines

App discovery via mobile travels two distinct paths.

**ASO optimizes app stores** via title, keywords, description, icon, screenshots and reviews. It's an iterative process where each element influences ranking.

**Mobile SEO optimizes search results**: Google App Packs, in-app indexation, landing pages and Schema.org markup. This is the web gateway to the app.

Key figures: 70% of installations come from store searches (ASO), but 27% of store traffic comes from web search. "Best app for..." queries surged 65% between 2024 and 2026. A **siloed** strategy leaves opportunities unexploited.
