## A niche but strategic market

**WordPress** currently powers over 43% of websites worldwide. Paradoxically, this dominance conceals a complex reality: optimizing a WordPress site requires highly specific competencies. Categories, tags, author archives, SEO plugin management, permalink structure, advanced structured data integration, security, all represent challenges that generalist SEO agencies often only partially master.

Add to this a new dimension since 2026: visibility within **generative search engines**. Citation rates in AI Overviews have surged 527%, and brands embedded in these answers capture 35% additional organic clicks. Deploying this strategy on WordPress demands fine-grained understanding of the platform combined with innovative thinking about organic positioning.

## Budgets and expected gains

WordPress SEO engagement unfolds across multiple investment levels.

A **complete audit mission** (without ongoing support) spans 2-3 weeks for a one-time cost of 1,500 to 4,000 euros. It delivers precise mapping of technical dysfunctions and opportunities.

For **smaller sites** (under 50 pages), standard engagement requires 1,000 to 2,500 euros monthly over 3-6 months. The scope covers on-page optimization, navigation structure, and basic technical audit.

An **advanced strategy** (50-200 pages) rises to 2,500-5,000 euros monthly over 4-8 months. Elaborate structured data, orchestrated internal linking, and GEO assessment enter the scope.

For **large-scale projects** (200+ pages, WordPress multisite, complex WooCommerce), budgets reach 5,000-10,000 euros monthly or more, with 4-10 month horizons. This tier covers holistic organic transformation vision.

**CMS migration** (to WordPress) is invoiced 5,000-15,000 euros as flat fee over 1-3 months. Substantial technical reengineering occurs here.

Beyond raw pricing, KPIs to track during engagement remain monthly organic traffic growth, average position evolution, increased presence in AI Overviews and ChatGPT, Core Web Vitals improvement, and organic visitor conversion rate.

## Which agency for which project

An **editorial site or WordPress blog** naturally fits specialist agencies like SeoMix or Noiise, where editorial architecture technical expertise dominates.

A **brochure site seeking scaling** benefits from **data-driven philosophy** of agencies like datashake or SmartKeyword, capable of maximizing organic ROI and integrating GEO from the start.

A **WooCommerce or substantial product catalog** requires agencies mastering Product schemas, complex e-commerce architectures (datashake, Peak Ace, Eskimoz).

**International presence** demands WPML/Polylang expertise and multidomain hreflang capability (Peak Ace, Eskimoz).

**Headless WordPress** (REST API exposed to React or Next.js frontend) requires top-tier technical capacity (Peak Ace, datashake).
