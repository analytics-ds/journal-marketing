> **In short:**
> 1. Retail media surpasses TV advertising in investment in several European markets.
> 2. Retailers' transactional data offers targeting unavailable elsewhere.
> 3. The lever remains under-used by brands outside consumer goods.

**Retail media**, advertising on retailers' sites and apps, has become the third pillar of online advertising, behind search and social.

## Why it is rising

- End of third-party cookies: retailers' first-party data becomes strategic
- Proximity to purchase: the ad shows at decision time
- Closed-loop measurement: impression, click and sale in the same ecosystem

## Who should go

Brands sold through retail first. But marketplaces open the lever to almost every e-merchant.

## Where to start

A test on a flagship range, a framed budget and an honest read of incrementality. The audit reflexes remain the same as on [Google Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-budget-settings/).
