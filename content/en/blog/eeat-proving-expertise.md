> **In short:**
> 1. First-hand experience has become the first E of the acronym, and the most discriminating.
> 2. Author pages linked to real profiles strengthen the whole domain.
> 3. Sourced citations and original data are the evidence engines read best.

**E-E-A-T** is not a score but a bundle of clues. The point is not to claim expertise, it is to make it verifiable.

## Evidence, not promises

- Author bylines with bio and references
- Sources cited with date and link
- Original data or field feedback

## The role of author pages

A solid author page links every article to an identifiable person, with their background and topics. It is a site-wide signal.

## Experience above all

Testing what you write about changes the writing: concrete details, honest limits, precise vocabulary. It is the natural extension of [an evergreen strategy](/en/blog/evergreen-content-method/).
