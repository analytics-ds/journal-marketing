## Understanding the programmatic market in 2026

### Programmatic as the backbone of display

In 2026, **programmatic advertising** represents over 85% of display investment in France. Real-time automated buying (RTB) has replaced direct negotiations for virtually all campaigns, offering unprecedented targeting precision and operational efficiency.

### Challenges in today's context

The ecosystem reorganizes around four major movements: the **programmed disappearance of third-party cookies** drives agencies and advertisers toward first-party data and contextual targeting; the **rise of CTV and audio** opens massive distribution channels to engaged audiences; **digital billboards (DOOH) become programmatic**, enabling targeted and measurable campaigns; **artificial intelligence transforms** bid optimization, creative and audience strategies.

### Programmatic and GEO synergy

Two approaches complement each other: programmatic generates visibility and qualified traffic, while **[GEO](/en/blog/what-is-geo-guide/)** ensures presence in generative AI engines. Brands combining these two levers maximize their digital share of voice. To dive deeper, explore how to optimize your visibility in AI engines.

## Selection criteria for a programmatic agency

### Essential competencies to verify

- **Multi-DSP operation**: ability to activate DV360, The Trade Desk, Amazon DSP, Xandr according to strategy
- **Data management**: building and activating first-party audiences
- **AI-powered optimization**: algorithms for bids, targeting and creative
- **Multi-touch attribution**: incrementality measurement beyond raw media KPIs
- **Dynamic creative**: automated ad personalization (DCO)
- **Financial transparency**: breakdown of tech costs, agency margins and placement quality

### Reliability indicators

- Current Google or The Trade Desk Partner certifications
- Documented client cases with real ROAS and CPA metrics
- Clear pricing (media costs, tech fees, agency fees)
- Documented brand safety and verification processes
- Expertise in data structuring to enrich audience strategies

## How to choose based on your profile

**SMBs discovering programmatic**: opt for a multi-channel agency capable of integrating programmatic into global strategy (datashake, Ad's up Consulting).

**Advertiser with significant media budget**: select a pure-play programmatic player with advanced algorithms (Gamned!, Tradelab).

**E-commerce and ROI**: choose a performance-driven agency with dynamic retargeting (Keyade, Ad's up Consulting).
