> **In short:**
> 1. An evergreen piece targets a question that is stable over time, not a news item.
> 2. A yearly update extends its lifespan by 2 to 3 years.
> 3. Internal linking turns an isolated article into a durable asset.

**Evergreen content** is the most profitable asset of an editorial strategy: it depreciates slowly and updates quickly.

## Choose subjects that do not expire

Good evergreen questions start with how, why, how much. They existed five years ago and will exist in five years.

## Write to last

- Dated, sourced facts rather than trends
- Examples that can be replaced without rewriting the article
- A heading structure that follows the logic of the question

## Maintain the asset

An evergreen piece is maintained like a garden: one pass a year, refreshed data, checked links. It is also the backbone of [E-E-A-T proof of expertise](/en/blog/eeat-proving-expertise/).
