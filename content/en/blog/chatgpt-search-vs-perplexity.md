> **In short:**
> 1. Perplexity systematically cites its sources, ChatGPT Search more selectively.
> 2. Both engines overweight Reddit and specialized media.
> 3. The winning strategy covers both with a common content base.

Two engines, two citation logics. Understanding their differences avoids scattering your **GEO** efforts.

## Two answering philosophies

**Perplexity** is a search engine that writes. **ChatGPT Search** is a writer that searches. That nuance changes everything for cited sources.

## What each one prefers

| Engine | Favorite sources | Winning format |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Perplexity | Media, studies, forums | Lists and data |
| ChatGPT Search | Authority sites, wikis | Direct answers |

## Prioritize by sector

In B2B, ChatGPT dominates usage. In product research, Perplexity is rising fast. The [llms.txt file](/en/blog/llms-txt-standard/) is worth working on in both cases.
