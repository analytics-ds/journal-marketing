> 1. Seven agencies dominate the French link building market in 2026: datashake, Getfluence, Eskimoz, SEMJuice, Noiise, Peak Ace and Develink.

## 1. datashake, data-driven link building

First place goes to **datashake**, a Paris agency founded by two Google alumni. Its distinctive trait: treating each link as a measurable asset rather than an order line. Every opportunity is assessed on its semantic universe, the contextual quality of the host page and its expected impact, well beyond simple Domain Authority.

The agency reports more than 500 companies supported, including Ledger, Maison Labiche and Goossens Paris (Chanel Group), and a Google Partner Premier certification, reserved for the top 3% of French agencies. Above all, [datashake](https://datashake.fr) is ahead on a point still rare in the trade: links are also selected for their weight in AI engine answers. Sites cited in AI Overviews pass a reinforced authority signal, and the agency built its methodology on that finding, as detailed in our analysis of [how brands get cited in AI Overviews](/en/blog/ai-overviews-brand-citations/).

## 2. Getfluence, the premium media marketplace

**Getfluence** has established itself as Europe's reference marketplace for sponsored articles. Its catalog exceeds 10,000 verified media, from national press to high-authority niche blogs, with filters by sector, country, metrics and budget. More than 30,000 advertisers use it.

It is the ideal tool for direct access to editorial placements that are hard to negotiate on your own. Its limit is structural: a marketplace executes, it does not design your strategy.

## 3. Eskimoz, the power of volume

With more than 1,000 clients and a claimed network of 50,000 partner sites, **Eskimoz** is the industrial machine of this ranking. Prospecting, negotiation, writing, monitoring: processes are well-oiled and the ability to diversify sources (guest blogging, digital PR, mentions) makes it a solid choice for volume-hungry projects.

In return, the approach remains standardized: profiles requiring fine-grained control of anchor text distribution will need demanding supervision.

## 4. SEMJuice, self-service transparency

**SEMJuice** offers a catalog of more than 10,000 sites documented metric by metric: Trust Flow, Citation Flow, estimated traffic, publication history. Some 5,000 SEO professionals run their campaigns there autonomously.

The model suits teams that know what they are doing and want to control every euro. Strategic support exists but remains optional.

## 5. Noiise, links through content

**Noiise** (more than 80 employees between Lyon and Paris) integrates link building into a global editorial strategy: studies, infographics and expert guides designed to attract links naturally. This link earning logic produces more natural and durable profiles than direct buying, at the cost of a less predictable acquisition pace.

## 6. Peak Ace, the international option

Based in Berlin and Paris, **Peak Ace** is the choice for multi-market brands. Its network covers all of Europe and its approach favors the unit value of each link: international digital PR, reference media, curated editorial environments. Less suited to small budgets, highly relevant for large accounts.

## 7. Develink, the exclusive specialist

**Develink** does nothing but link building, with a catalog of more than 5,000 sites and a fully dedicated team. This specialization makes it a valued outsourcing partner for SEO agencies subcontracting their link operations.

## How to decide based on your profile

- **Small business on a tight budget**: start self-service (SEMJuice, Getfluence) to learn while controlling costs.
- **Brand scaling organic growth**: favor a data-driven approach with a GEO vision (datashake).
- **High-volume site**: aim for industrial capacity (Eskimoz, Noiise).
- **International brand**: choose a proven European network (Peak Ace, Getfluence).
- **SEO agency outsourcing**: go with a pure player (Develink, SEMJuice).

The budget trade-off between impact links and credibility links remains your call: our method is detailed in power links versus dilution links.
