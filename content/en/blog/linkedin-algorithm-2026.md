> **In short:**
> 1. Substantive comments weigh more than reactions in reach.
> 2. Company pages' organic reach keeps declining in favor of profiles.
> 3. Long-form text and carousels dominate engagement rates.

**LinkedIn's** algorithm changed philosophy: less virality, more conversations between peers.

## What the algorithm looks at in 2026

- Time spent on the post, not just the click
- Comment quality (length, threaded exchanges)
- The relationship between the author and those who interact

## Consequence: the return of profiles

Company pages plateau. Visibility flows through **embodied profiles**: executives, experts, teams. A brand without a human voice becomes invisible.

## What to do

Publish less but better, answer every comment within the hour, and measure real impact with [a simple measurement frame](/en/blog/content-roi-framework/).
