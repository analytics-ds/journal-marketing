> **In short:**
> 1. Daily update frequency has become the market standard.
> 2. Tracking enriched SERPs (AI Overviews, featured snippets) makes the difference.
> 3. Price gaps range from 1 to 5 for similar features.

**Rank tracking** remains SEO's daily dashboard. But not all trackers are equal since AI answers entered the SERPs.

## The criteria that matter

- Reading freshness (daily or weekly)
- SERP feature tracking, including AI Overviews
- Fine-grained geolocation
- Exports and API for reporting

## The comparison table

| Criterion | Tool A | Tool B | Tool C |
|-----------|--------|--------|--------|
| Daily readings | Yes | Yes | Optional |
| AI Overviews tracking | Yes | No | Yes |
| Indicative monthly price | 90 euros | 40 euros | 120 euros |
