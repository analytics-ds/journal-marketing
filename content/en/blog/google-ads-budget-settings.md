> **In short:**
> 1. The display network enabled by default on search campaigns absorbs up to 20% of budget.
> 2. Broad queries without negative keywords dilute relevance.
> 3. A quarterly search terms audit is enough to fix most of it.

**Google Ads** accounts rarely lose money on strategy. They lose it on default settings never questioned.

## The 5 settings to check

1. Display network ticked on search campaigns
2. Automatic keyword broadening
3. Automatic placements without exclusions
4. Smart bidding without a reliable conversion goal
5. Automatically generated extensions

## The quick audit method

One hour per quarter: search terms report, sort by spend, exclude everything that does not match intent. Tracking must be reliable upstream, see [our useful GA4 reports](/en/blog/ga4-seo-reports/).
