> **In short:**
> 1. The landing pages report crossed with Search Console remains the pivot of SEO analysis.
> 2. Key events replace conversions and change how ROI is read.
> 3. Free-form explorations let you rebuild content cohorts.

**GA4** has a reputation for being unreadable. In reality, seven reports are enough to steer a site's organic performance.

## The fundamental reports

- Landing pages with organic filter
- Traffic acquisition by channel
- Key events by entry page

## Crossing with Search Console

The native GA4 and Search Console link adds queries to the dashboard. That is where you read the gap between **impressions** and **sessions**, in other words the untapped CTR potential.
