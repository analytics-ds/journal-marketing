> **In short:**
> 1. Micro-creators show engagement rates 2 to 3 times higher than big accounts.
> 2. Niche proximity weighs more than audience size in conversion.
> 3. Cost per sale is often lower despite reduced unit fees.

**Micro-influence** is no longer a budget plan B: it is the most effective segment of influencer marketing.

## Why it converts

- A niche audience that trusts the recommendation
- A direct creator-community link (replies to comments, DMs)
- Content perceived as authentic, not as placements

## How to select

Look at real engagement rate, topical consistency and comment quality. An account of 20,000 engaged followers beats one of 200,000 passive spectators.

## Measure honestly

Dedicated promo codes, tracked links and a realistic attribution window: measurement is the weak link of the lever, as often, see [the ROI framework](/en/blog/content-roi-framework/).
