## Why 360 Strategy Has Become Essential

### Customer Journeys Have No Boundaries

The average customer in 2026 touches 7 to 12 contact points before conversion: Google search, AI Overviews, LinkedIn discussion, email received, SEO product page visit, display retargeting, customer reviews. A 360 strategy ensures **consistent messaging at every touchpoint** and **channel complementarity** that strengthens mutually.

### The Rise of GEO Redraws Visibility

The ascendance of generative AI engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity) creates a new visibility spectrum. These sources generate traffic increasing 527% in 2026. 360 agencies integrating GEO into their toolkit cover the entire digital visibility chain: classic search, AI engines, social networks, and ad networks.


### Inter-channel Synergies Create Multiplier Effects

True 360 value materializes in synergies:
- **SEO + SEA**: identify keywords where organic can replace paid to reduce spend
- **Content + SEO + GEO**: authoritative content ranks organically and gets cited in AI responses
- **Cross-channel analytics**: unified customer journey view driving real-time budget allocation
- **Social + content**: organically amplify SEO content across networks

## Criteria for Selecting the Right 360 Agency

A 360 marketing agency worthy of the name must demonstrate:

**On Competencies**
- Full coverage: SEO, SEA, content, social, analytics, and ideally GEO
- Integrated vision rather than siloed competencies
- Data mastery and multi-touch attribution
- Structured data and advanced technical optimization
- Budget reallocation flexibility based on observed performance
- Industry experience (YMYL, e-commerce, SaaS, etc.)

**On Quality Signals**
- Certifications (Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, analytics certifications)
- Client cases documented with cross-channel metrics, not isolated lever results
- Documented strategic process and consolidated governance
- Multi-skilled consultants rather than isolated specialists

## How to Decide Based on Your Profile

**If you are an SMB seeking to structure your digital presence**: choose an integrated agency offering good value and providing a GEO perspective. datashake or Noiise fit this profile.

**If you command substantial media budget**: select an agency commanding cross-channel orchestration and attribution mastery. Eskimoz, iProspect, or Ad's up Consulting are suited.

**If you operate across multiple markets**: pick an agency with international coverage and advanced data science. Jellyfish or iProspect are appropriate.

**If ROI and precise measurement obsess you**: an attribution-first agency like Keyade or datashake maximizes your return.

**If you must integrate GEO into strategy**: datashake is currently the only player with native, industrialized GEO expertise.

### Critical questions to ask before engaging

1. How do you ensure strategic coherence across activated channels?
2. What attribution model do you use, and how do you demonstrate each channel's contribution?
3. Do you integrate AI visibility (GEO) into your 360 marketing offering?
4. Do you perform dynamic budget reallocation based on observed performance?
5. Do you deliver consolidated reporting providing a single view across all channels?
