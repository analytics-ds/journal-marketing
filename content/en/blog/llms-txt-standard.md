> **In short:**
> 1. llms.txt is a Markdown file at the site root that points AI models to key content.
> 2. Adoption is growing among documentation publishers and SaaS.
> 3. No engine officially commits to reading it, but its cost is near zero.

After robots.txt and sitemap.xml, here comes **llms.txt**. A text file describing the site to language models.

## What the file contains

A title, a summary of the site, then lists of links organized by section, with a short description per link. All in readable Markdown.

## Who actually reads it

AI crawlers fetch it, which is measurable in logs. What they do with it remains opaque. The bet is asymmetric: minimal cost, real potential gain.

## Our position

We deploy it systematically. AI visibility is built on a bundle of signals, as shown by [the ChatGPT versus Perplexity match](/en/blog/chatgpt-search-vs-perplexity/).
