> **In short:**
> 1. Generalist suites cover 80% of everyday production needs.
> 2. Specialized tools keep the edge on video and editing.
> 3. Real cost is measured in proofreading time, not subscription price.

The **AI creation tools** market has consolidated: fewer players, more complete suites, and a real gap between leaders and the rest.

## The comparison in one table

| Tool | Strength | Limit | From |
|------|----------|-------|------|
| Generalist suite A | Text and image versatility | Basic video | 20 euros/month |
| Writing specialist B | Brand tone | No image | 30 euros/month |
| Video studio C | Automatic editing | Learning curve | 45 euros/month |

## How to choose

- Start from the formats produced each week, not the promises
- Test output quality in your language, it varies widely
- Measure the proofreading time needed before publishing

## The real criterion: proofreading

A text generated in 30 seconds that requires 20 minutes of correction saves nothing. Real gains come from [the evergreen method](/en/blog/evergreen-content-method/) and brief preparation.
