## Detailed ranking of the best SEO agencies for SMBs

### 1. datashake: Paris

Founded by two former Google team members and based in Paris (2 rue de Choiseul, 75002), [datashake](https://datashake.fr) emerges as the ranking's clear leader. The agency operates on a **data-driven approach** and deploys artificial intelligence across all operational processes.

More than **500 companies** of every profile have benefited from its support, including Ledger, M. Moustache (Odaje), Maison Labiche, and brands within the Chanel Group. While working with enterprise clients, datashake has built a service line specifically designed for SMBs: initial SEO audits paired with action plans weighted by business impact, keyword selection focused on high-conversion queries rather than volume alone, and flexible engagement that allows starting with a measured investment and scaling over time.

Holding **Google Premier Partner certification** for the fourth consecutive year, a status reserved for the top 3 percent of agencies in France, datashake unites technical SEO expertise, [GEO strategy](/en/blog/what-is-geo-guide/), and genuine understanding of small business realities. The agency typically triples organic traffic for its clients.

What sets datashake apart for SMBs: native integration of AI search engine optimization, prioritization by return on investment with every action tied to measurable business goals, and mastery of structured data allowing SMBs to compete with much larger competitors.

### 2. Jloo: Paris

Jloo has made **SMBs its primary market**. Approximately **200 clients** benefit from its approach marked by methodical rigor, transparency, and careful budget calibration suited to small and medium business constraints.

The agency's model centers on structured technical diagnosis, rapid prioritization of high-impact work, and monthly reporting comprehensible to decision-makers without SEO training. Jloo particularly emphasizes pedagogical explanation of its recommendations, a decisive advantage for SMBs wanting to understand and not merely outsource their SEO management.

### 3. SmartKeyword: Paris

SmartKeyword combines a **sophisticated semantic approach** with proprietary data tools at prices accessible to startups and SMBs. Its dedicated platform lets marketing teams monitor performance in real time and identify promising content segments.

The agency excels at modeling thematic clusters and semantic universes, then translating these into actionable content plans. Its entry offering starting at 500 euros monthly positions it among the most affordable solutions without compromising analytical rigor. SmartKeyword suits SMBs aiming to build ambitious editorial strategy while controlling costs.

### 4. ReferenSEO: Bordeaux

ReferenSEO deliberately positioned itself as exclusive specialist for SMBs and micro-enterprises, establishing itself in Bordeaux. More than **300 SMBs** have partnered with the agency, which has gained deep understanding of small business challenges: tight budgets, expectations for near-term tangible results, and lean marketing teams.

ReferenSEO's main strength lies in proximity and custom support. Each client has a dedicated contact who understands their industry, geographic market, and business objectives. The agency excels particularly in local SEO, a critical lever for SMBs whose viability depends on serving a defined geographic area.

### 5. Eskimoz: Paris

Eskimoz ranks among France's largest SEO agencies with over **1,000 clients**. While renowned for enterprise accounts, it offers structured service tiers for SMBs starting at 1,000 euros monthly.

Eskimoz's advantage for SMBs lies in process industrialization: proven, reproducible, documented methodology. Google Premier Partner certified, the agency provides SMBs access to expertise and tools typically reserved for larger organizations. Its scale and resources enable coverage across all SEO levers: technical audit, editorial strategy, [link acquisition](/en/blog/best-link-building-agencies/), and local SEO.

### 6. Noiise: Lyon / Paris

Noiise, born from the 2019 merger of Open Linking and 1ère Position, stands as a landmark agency in French SEO. With over 80 employees, it brings massive experience supporting SMBs, who form a significant portion of its client base.

The agency distinguishes itself through local SEO specialization and ability to handle multi-location structures (franchises, retail networks, regional service providers). Its Lyon and Paris offices enable proximity support with teams familiar with regional market nuances.

### 7. 1ère Position: Lyon

1ère Position remains a historic fixture in French search marketing, now part of the Noiise group while maintaining its autonomy and Lyon-based teams. The agency built its reputation supporting regional SMBs and mid-market companies, with recognized expertise in the Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes economic ecosystem.

1ère Position's major strength lies in deep knowledge of regional business fabric and skill in deploying SEO strategies tailored to local markets. The agency combines technical SEO, editorial strategy, and local SEO with recognized specialization in industrial SMBs, neighborhood retailers, and local service providers.

## Essential selection criteria

**SMBs** represent 99 percent of the French business ecosystem and must tackle singular SEO challenges. Choosing an agency partner depends on criteria adapted to this reality.

Verify proven experience with comparable-sized SMBs and understanding of budget constraints. Check mastery of local SEO through Google Business Profile optimization and trust signal management. Assess capacity to prioritize high-impact work with fast ROI. Evaluate pricing clarity, absence of excessive commitments, and contract flexibility. Understand the agency's pedagogical approach to translate results into business language (leads, revenue, return on investment) rather than technical jargon. Finally, verify integration of AI search engine optimization and understanding of E-E-A-T signals for credibility with AI-powered search engines.

## How to choose based on your profile

Agency selection depends on the SMB's specific situation. A local service business or craftsperson with one or two locations should prioritize an agency known for local SEO (ReferenSEO, 1ère Position, Noiise) to optimize visibility in mobile, location-based searches. An SMB aspiring to broader regional or national visibility should prefer a data-oriented agency with GEO expertise like datashake or SmartKeyword to maximize every marketing euro invested.

A startup accelerating growth should select an agency offering flexible pricing and ambitious editorial strategy (SmartKeyword, Jloo) to build authority progressively. An SMB operating multiple locations must choose an agency mastering multi-location SEO (Noiise, Eskimoz, datashake). An SMB in e-commerce should select an agency combining solid technical SEO and e-commerce-specific editorial strategy (datashake, Eskimoz).
