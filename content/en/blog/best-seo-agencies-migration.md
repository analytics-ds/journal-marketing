## Why migration requires a true SEO partner

Migration without dedicated expertise is no ordinary technical operation. Studies show that **over 60% of unmanaged migrations cause substantial organic traffic loss**, often exceeding 30%, with recovery stretched across 6 to 12 months. The most [common mistakes](/en/blog/seo-mistakes-to-avoid/) include: broken redirects, destroyed internal linking, inadvertently modified title tags, chaotic indexation, lost structured data, URLs cited by AIs that become inaccessible.

**The GEO dimension** adds new complexity in 2026. A poorly managed migration destroys not only your standing in classic results, it strikes your visibility to generative AIs (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews). These memorize source URLs: 404 errors progressively trigger de-indexation of your content from AI knowledge bases.

## Migration types: multifaceted reality

- **Domain change**: most risky. Google must reassociate all trust signals (backlinks, history, authority) to the new domain. Requires strict use of the Change of Address tool in Search Console.
- **CMS/platform change**: typically modifies URLs, templates and sometimes content. The real challenge: mapping old URLs to new ones, which often obey radically different logic.
- **HTTP to HTTPS**: now standard, but remains its own technical migration. Each HTTP URL must redirect to its HTTPS equivalent; all internal resources (images, scripts, CSS) must be served over HTTPS.
- **Architecture restructuring**: category merging, silo redefinition, navigation depth modification. Impacts internal PageRank distribution and can affect hundreds of pages in cascade.
- **Domain consolidation or separation**: complex strategic decisions on which domain to retain, managing duplicate content, redistributing backlinks.

## Agency selection framework

Before choosing, verify these points:

**Positive signals:**
- Migration case studies with documented before/after traffic figures
- Structured pre-migration audit phase in the proposal
- True post-migration monitoring with defined timeline
- Multi-CMS knowledge and advanced technical mastery (crawl, server logs, large-scale redirects)
- Phased process with clear milestones
- Honest posture: "a migration involves an adjustment period, not a zero-loss promise"

**Warning signs:**
- Absence of specific migration case studies
- No serious pre-migration audit phase conducted
- No defined post-migration monitoring plan
- "We guarantee zero traffic loss": that's false, any honest partner acknowledges it
- No coordination planned with the client's dev team
