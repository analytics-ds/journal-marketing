> **In short:**
> 1. AI Overviews appear on nearly 30% of informational queries in France.
> 2. Content structured as questions and answers gets cited twice as often.
> 3. Source authority matters more than classic organic position.
> 4. A brand cited in the AI answer captures attention before the first blue link.

Visibility is no longer decided in the ten blue links alone. Google's **AI Overviews** compose a direct, sourced answer, placed above everything else. Getting cited in that answer has become a goal in itself.

## Why AI Overviews change the game

The click reflex fades when the answer is already on screen. For a brand, the challenge shifts: it is no longer only about ranking, but about being **the source the AI chooses to cite**.

### What the data shows

- Cited pages are not always in the organic top 3.
- List, table and FAQ formats dominate citations.
- Content freshness plays a measurable role on trending queries.

## How models pick their sources

Generative engines favor **self-contained** passages: a complete idea, simply worded, extractable without its context.

| Criterion | Observed weight | Good practice |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| Direct answer at the top of a section | High | One answer sentence under each heading |
| Sourced, quantified data | High | Cite the study and the year |
| Descriptive heading structure | Medium | Headings that state the question |
| Domain authority | Medium | Link building and brand mentions |

## The method in 4 steps

1. Identify the prompts where the brand should appear.
2. Map the sources currently cited by AIs.
3. Produce content that answers better than those sources.
4. Measure citations earned and iterate every month.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How long does it take to get cited in an AI Overview?</summary>

On niche queries, citations sometimes appear within weeks. On competitive queries, expect several months of combined content and authority work.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you need content dedicated to AI engines?</summary>

No. Good GEO content remains good SEO content: structured, factual, sourced. Only the starting intent changes, you start from the prompt rather than the keyword.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are AI Overviews killing organic traffic?</summary>

They reduce it on purely informational queries, but the remaining clicks are more qualified. Brand citations make up in awareness what is lost in volume.

</details>
