> **In short:**
> 1. A query alternating between two URLs in Search Console is the classic symptom.
> 2. Merging content solves the majority of detected cases.
> 3. The most effective safeguard sits before publication, in the editorial roadmap.

**Cannibalization** is a silent problem: no error, no message, just rankings that plateau.

## Recognize the symptoms

- Two URLs alternating on the same query
- An abnormally low CTR despite a good average position
- Impressions split between sibling pages

## The remedies, by frequency

1. Merge content and redirect
2. Differentiate search intents
3. Canonicalize when pages must coexist

## Prevent rather than cure

Upstream control of the editorial roadmap costs one minute per topic. It avoids months of plateauing, and preserves [crawl budget](/en/blog/crawl-budget-5-signals/).
