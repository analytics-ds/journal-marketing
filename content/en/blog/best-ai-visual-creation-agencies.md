## The 7 best agencies in detail

### 1. datashake: The integrated SEO and GEO approach

datashake tops this ranking for its strategic uniqueness. Founded by former Google employees and based in Paris, the agency merges AI visual creation with organic optimization logic (SEO) and visibility with generative search engines (GEO).

Beyond image generation via Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion and Flux, datashake applies a complete process: **metadata optimization** (semantic alt texts, Schema.org ImageObject markup, WebP formats), intelligent deployment and performance tracking. This approach makes each visual productive in terms of visibility.

The agency has supported over 500 companies including brands like Ledger, Odaje and Goossens. Certified as a Google Premier Partner for 4 consecutive years, [datashake](https://datashake.fr) is the only agency in this ranking offering dedicated GEO expertise with visuals designed for [appearance in Google AI Overviews](/en/blog/what-is-geo-guide/). To deepen this approach, explore its expertise in structured data and Schema.org.

### 2. Artefact: Excellence in industrialization

Artefact is a consulting firm specializing in data and artificial intelligence that has built solid expertise in **large-scale AI visual production**. The agency has supported over 200 clients in their AI transformation and stands out for its ability to create automated pipelines.

Artefact's process converts structured briefs into series of visuals automatically validated against brand guidelines, then generates variants for A/B testing. The agency also develops **fine-tuned models** trained on each client's specific assets, guaranteeing brand coherence and authenticity.

### 3. Brainsonic: The fusion of human creativity and AI

Brainsonic combines content marketing and creation by integrating generative tools at the heart of its production. With 300 clients, the agency does not treat AI as a replacement tool but as a **extension of artistic direction**.

Its creative directors use Midjourney, DALL-E 3 and Adobe Firefly as genuine creative tools, with validation and refinement at each stage. This philosophy ensures visuals that combine AI speed with the creative demands of a high-end traditional agency.

### 4. Publicis Sapient: Group resources for major accounts

Publicis Sapient, the digital transformation branch of the Publicis group, has the means to deploy **AI projects at very large scale**. The agency primarily serves major accounts requiring custom solutions and complete industrialization.

Its strengths: development of proprietary models trained on brand assets, implementation of automated production chains capable of generating thousands of variants, integration of the AI pipeline into the client's existing marketing systems.

### 5. Kamp'n: AI visuals and social media performance

Kamp'n specializes in optimizing advertising performance, integrating generative AI as a lever to improve campaigns. With 400 clients, the agency uses AI visuals to multiply A/B tests.

Its approach is resolutely **data-driven**: generating dozens of visual variants per campaign, continuous A/B testing, rapid iteration to maximize CTR and conversion. This methodology reduces cost per acquisition by 20 to 30% compared to traditional static creations.

### 6. Eskimoz: Industrial production and high volume

Eskimoz has integrated AI visual creation into its **large-scale content offerings**. With over 1,000 clients, the agency prioritizes production efficiency: quickly generated visuals, web-optimized, marked up for SEO.

This agency is particularly suited for companies with high visual content needs to fuel intensive SEO content strategies, regular blogs or multilingual campaigns.

### 7. Yllias: Premium creativity and advanced artistic direction

Yllias is a **creative studio specializing in generative AI** based in Lyon. The team comprises creative directors and prompt engineers, with access to advanced workflows (ComfyUI, post-AI retouching).

The studio aims for artistic quality above production volume: complex prompting, fine parameter control, retouching to reach levels close to professional photography. Suited to premium brands seeking unique and artistically distinct visuals.

## Comparative analysis: selection criteria

### Key competencies to verify

Before signing, validate that the agency masters:

- **Tools**: Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion, advanced workflows (ComfyUI, ControlNet)
- **Artistic direction**: converting briefs into prompts and post-processing
- **Visual coherence**: model fine-tuning, AI guidelines development
- **Technical optimization**: alt texts, Schema.org, web formats, particularly for generative AI optimization tools
- **Scalability**: production capacity while maintaining quality
- **Legal compliance**: management of usage rights

## How to choose according to your profile

**Company seeking visuals plus SEO optimization**: choose datashake for a holistic approach integrating organic visibility.

**SME with limited budget**: start with free tools or a specialized provider like Yllias for targeted projects.

**Brand with high social media ad needs**: opt for Kamp'n, capable of massively A/B testing visual creations.

**Large enterprise undergoing transformation**: Publicis Sapient and Artefact offer the expertise and resources for complex projects.

**Premium brand demanding uniqueness**: Yllias and Brainsonic will create distinctive and artistically coherent visuals.

## Essential questions before contracting

1. Which **AI tools** do you master and how do you ensure brand consistency?
2. How do you optimize visuals for **organic visibility** (alt text, Schema.org, formats)?
3. Do you offer **model fine-tuning** on our own assets?
4. What is your **production capacity** per month and standard timeline?
5. How do you manage **usage rights** of AI-generated images?
