## Why Shopify requires a different SEO approach

**Shopify** represents the strategic choice of over **26% of online stores worldwide**, with revenue projections exceeding **12 billion dollars in 2026**. In France, this growing adoption of Shopify stores creates strong demand for specialized SEO expertise.

Unlike generalist CMS platforms, Shopify imposes a **rigid technical architecture** that creates specific challenges: URLs must follow `/collections/` and `/products/` patterns, product pages generate unintended duplicates, the `robots.txt` file offers limited control, and the **Liquid** template system requires in-depth knowledge to correctly integrate semantic markup and structured data.

Beyond traditional SEO, Shopify merchants face an emerging yet strategic challenge: **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization). In 2025, references generated by AI engines surged by **527%**, and brands cited in Google's AI Overviews responses capture **35% additional clicks** compared to non-cited brands. For e-commerce merchants, appearing in responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overview becomes a distinctive visibility lever.

## Criteria for selecting a reliable Shopify SEO agency

### Non-negotiable technical competencies

A competent agency must demonstrate proven mastery in these areas:

- In-depth knowledge of Liquid and Shopify themes
- Management of **canonical URLs** and URL structure patterns
- E-commerce-oriented content strategy: product pages, categories, blog
- Core Web Vitals and **structured data Product + BreadcrumbList**
- E-commerce targeted link building and **GEO / AI**

### Quality signals to verify

- Client portfolio documented specifically on Shopify (not generalist)
- Detailed case studies with quantified metrics
- Official certifications (Google Partner, Shopify Partner)
- Clear reporting process and defined frequency
- Demonstrated understanding of structured data applied to e-commerce

According to a 2026 report, **85% of companies plan to increase investment in structured data and Schema.org** to strengthen their visibility in responses from generative search engines.

## Assessment and selection approach

### Clarify needs before prospecting

Before contacting an agency, formulate precisely:

- **Commercial objective**: traffic growth, conversion improvement, geographic expansion
- **Budget framework**: monthly envelope and intended engagement duration
- **Site context**: SEO history, repositioning, or new launch
- **GEO ambition**: desire to integrate visibility strategy in AI search engines

### Critical questions to ask in pre-sales

During discussions, address these questions to assess real expertise:

- How many Shopify projects does the agency currently manage?
- What **concrete results** can it demonstrate on comparable projects?
- How does it navigate **Shopify technical limitations** (URLs, duplication, Liquid)?
- Does it integrate a **GEO strategy** within its traditional SEO offering?
- What is its reporting process and evaluation cadence?

Choosing a partner for Shopify SEO constitutes a strategic investment requiring an agency capable of combining **technical mastery**, **data orientation**, and **anticipation of SEO landscape mutations**. In a context where AI engines redefine how buyers discover brands, prioritizing an agency mastering both **traditional SEO** and **GEO** proves to be a sustainable competitive advantage.
