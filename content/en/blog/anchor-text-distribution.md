> **In short:**
> 1. Brand anchors should represent half of a healthy profile.
> 2. Exact-match anchors are reserved for pillar pages, sparingly.
> 3. An anchor audit happens before every campaign, never after.

The anchor is the clickable text of a link, and its dosage makes the difference between a natural profile and a suspicious one.

## The main anchor families

- **Brand**: the site or company name
- **Naked URL**: the raw address
- **Generic**: click here, learn more
- **Exact or partial match**: the target keyword

## The reference ratios

A balanced profile sits around 50% brand, 20% naked URLs and generics, 30% broader semantics of which only a fraction is exact match.

> "The perfect anchor is the one an editor would have written without us."
> Sophie Marchand

## Arbitrate with the budget

Anchor distribution should be planned together with [the power versus dilution trade-off](/en/blog/power-vs-dilution-links/): both decisions go hand in hand.
