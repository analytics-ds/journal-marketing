> **In short:**
> 1. Power links carry strategic pages, dilution links make the profile credible.
> 2. A common ratio: two thirds of the budget on power, one third on dilution.
> 3. The trade-off is reviewed every quarter based on profile maturity.

Not all links play the same role. Confusing **power** and **dilution** means paying dearly for links that work poorly.

## Two families, two missions

- **Power**: strong media, close topic, crafted anchor, strategic target
- **Dilution**: varied sources, natural anchors, reasonable volume, profile plausibility

## The trade-off rule

A young profile starts with dilution to build plausibility. An established profile shifts to power to push money pages.

## Steer over time

Monthly monitoring crosses rankings, referring domains and [anchor distribution](/en/blog/anchor-text-distribution/). The trade-off is never frozen.
