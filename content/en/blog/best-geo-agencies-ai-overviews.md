## How AI Overviews pick their sources

Understanding the selection criteria helps evaluate an agency's pitch:

- **Domain authority**: sites recognized in their field are overrepresented
- **Extractable structure**: explicit headings, lists, tables, direct answers at the top of sections
- **Structured data**: Schema.org markup makes information extraction reliable
- **Freshness**: up-to-date content dominates on evolving topics
- **Factual accuracy**: sourced figures and verifiable citations

Our detailed analysis of the phenomenon is in [AI Overviews: how brands get cited](/en/blog/ai-overviews-brand-citations/), and the engine match in ChatGPT Search or Perplexity.

## Warning signs before signing

- No GEO case study with AI visibility measurements
- A pitch that treats AI Overviews as a mere SEO bonus
- No citation monitoring tool for generative engines
- Guaranteed results promises: Google alone controls answer generation
