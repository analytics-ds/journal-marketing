## Why hire a specialized web analytics agency?

### Measure what truly matters

Analytics is not an end in itself: it's the tool for measuring the true ROI of your marketing investments. An agency structures your data collection to track not just traffic, but events, conversions and user journeys relevant to your business.

### Track new traffic sources

With the rise of GEO, AI assistants generate a growing share of organic traffic. Without properly configured **GA4** to track ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews, you remain blind to a strategic part of your traffic.


### Ensure regulatory compliance

Web data collection falls under RGPD and ePrivacy regulations. An agency ensures your implementation (consent, anonymization, CNIL compliance) poses no legal risks.

## Essential criteria for choosing your agency

- **GA4 mastery**: advanced configuration, custom events, BigQuery integration
- **Tag management**: GTM, consent mode, structured data layer
- **Actionable dashboards**: Looker Studio, Tableau or Power BI, not just reports
- **Certifications**: Google Partner, Adobe Analytics Certified
- **Documented case studies**: look for examples of measured business impact
- **Internal training**: does the agency train your teams for independence?
