> **In short:**
> 1. Four indicators are enough: coverage, capture, conversion, contribution.
> 2. A content's value is measured over twelve rolling months, not its publication month.
> 3. Full cost includes production, updates and internal linking.

Measuring **content ROI** does not require a data warehouse. It mostly requires a stable frame and shared definitions.

## The four levels of the framework

1. **Coverage**: share of target queries where the site is visible
2. **Capture**: organic sessions generated
3. **Conversion**: key events attributed to the pages
4. **Contribution**: estimated monetary value

## The right time horizon

An evergreen article reaches cruising speed between its sixth and twelfth month. Judging content after thirty days is like measuring a seed with a ruler.
